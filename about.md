# Practice Ghost

Race against yourself. Every normal-mode attempt is recorded, and your **best run** on each level plays back as a translucent ghost next to you.

- A completed run beats any death; a faster completion beats a slower one; otherwise the run that got furthest wins.
- The ghost shows your gamemode, size and gravity as they were at that moment.
- Ghosts are saved per level and survive restarts.
- Use the **trash button** in the pause menu to delete a level's ghost.

The ghost only appears in normal mode from the start of the level. Practice mode and start positions break the timing, so they are not recorded.

Settings: show/hide the ghost, opacity, and tint color.
